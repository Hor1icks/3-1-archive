A NOTE ON THE SOURCES

The archive, in context.

78 written question groups from 15 papers, plus two viva allocations. Shared scenarios and all subparts stay with their original question.

    How it is structured

    Questions are organized by chapter and topic, with the year, assessment, marks, and original source attached. A question may cover several chapters. Material outside the taught syllabus appears under Others.

    Choose multiple years, combine chapter and topic filters, or search using quoted terms such as "BCNF" "SQL injection" to find questions matching either phrase. Expand any result to read its complete scenario and subparts.

    Saved questions stay in this browser. There are no accounts, trackers, or paid search services.
